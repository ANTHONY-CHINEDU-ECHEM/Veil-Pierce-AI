## Findings

* Nominee director network (score 0.95). The director Sanjay Qureshi Desai holds 210 director appointments, 92 percent of them in shell like companies, and is the sole director of 92 percent. [E03][E07][M01]
* Guidance on this pattern: Volume on its own is not proof. Accountants and professional directors also hold many appointments. The combination of volume with disposable companies and sole directorships is what marks a nominee network. [T01]
* Mass registration address (score 0.90). The registered office is shared by 549 companies, 91 percent of which are shell like, with 87 percent of directors resident overseas. [E02][E08][M01]
* Guidance on this pattern: A registered office gives a shell an appearance of UK substance. A shared address with a high share of disposable companies is one of the strongest network level indicators available from public data. [T04]
* Opaque offshore ownership (score 0.97). The declared chain of control reaches no natural person. It ends at KINGFISHER TEAL FINANCE LTD, registered in British Virgin Islands. Leak data names Hannah Rachel Macleod behind the offshore owner, which the register does not show. [E04][E05][E06][E09][M01]
* Guidance on this pattern: Leak data can close a gap in the declared chain. It is also dated, incomplete and unverified, and appearing in it is not evidence of wrongdoing since offshore entities have lawful uses. [T16]

Reviewer steps for Nominee director network [T01]:

* Request evidence of who gives instructions to the director
* Treat every company fronted by the same nominee as one connected risk
* Apply enhanced due diligence before onboarding or continuing the relationship

Reviewer steps for Mass registration address [T04]:

* Treat the address as a risk factor for every company registered there
* Check whether the business has any real presence at the address
* Report suspected false addresses to the registrar

Reviewer steps for Opaque offshore ownership [T16]:

* Treat leak data as a lead to verify, never as proof
* Ask the customer to confirm or explain the relationship
* Record the source and date of the leaked record
