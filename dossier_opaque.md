## Findings

* Mass registration address (score 0.78). The registered office is shared by 743 companies, 70 percent of which are shell like, with 78 percent of directors resident overseas. [E02][E07][M01]
* Guidance on this pattern: A registered office gives a shell an appearance of UK substance. A shared address with a high share of disposable companies is one of the strongest network level indicators available from public data. [T04]
* Opaque offshore ownership (score 0.97). The declared chain of control reaches no natural person. It ends at RIVERSIDE DERWENT AGRI HOLDINGS LTD, registered in British Virgin Islands. Leak data names Reza Khalid Ahmadi behind the offshore owner, which the register does not show. [E04][E05][E06][E08][M01]
* Guidance on this pattern: Leak data can close a gap in the declared chain. It is also dated, incomplete and unverified, and appearing in it is not evidence of wrongdoing since offshore entities have lawful uses. [T16]
